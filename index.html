Our story

From Georgia's Kitchen to the Truck

Georgia's Greek began with Georgia Kazakos and her restaurant and deli at 323 NW 85th St, where her cooking fed Seattle for over 30 years.

Along the way, the restaurant was featured on Diners, Drive-Ins and Dives.

Today her son, chef and owner Laki Kazakos, carries the family recipes on Georgia's Greek Food Truck, on the road since 2021.

The menu

Classic Greek, Made Fresh

Georgia's family recipes, from the yeero to the spanakopita.

Yeero & souvlaki

  • Traditional Yeero

    Halal lamb and beef yeero meat wrapped in a warm pita with mixed greens, red onions and sliced tomatoes, topped with our housemade tzatziki.

  • Chicken Souvlaki

    Marinated halal chicken souvlaki wrapped in a warm pita with mixed greens, red onions and sliced tomatoes, topped with our housemade tzatziki.

  • Roasted Leg of Lamb

    Halal braised and roasted leg of lamb, thinly sliced and wrapped in a warm pita with greens, onions and tomatoes, topped with tzatziki and feta.

Spanakopita & dolmas

  • Bourekia Spanakopita

    Two triangles of spinach, myzithra and feta in fillo.

  • Bourekia Tyropita

    Triangles of myzithra and feta cheese in fillo.

  • Dolmathakia

    Vegetarian stuffed grape leaves with a side of tzatziki.

Falafel & hummus

  • Falafel

    Housemade falafel wrapped in warm pita with mixed greens, red onions and sliced tomatoes, topped with Georgia's traditional tzatziki.

  • Falafel Meze

    Housemade falafel with cherry tomatoes, cucumbers, carrots and tzatziki.

  • Hummus & Pita

    Chickpeas with tahini, garlic, olive oil and lemon, with warm pita bread.

  • Melitzanasalata & Skorthalia

    Roasted eggplant dip, and a garlic and potato dip, each with pita.

Salads

  • Greek Salad

    Mixed greens, red onions, cherry tomatoes, bell peppers, cucumbers, olives and crumbled feta with our housemade lemon, olive oil and herb dressing.

  • Make it a meal

    Top it with halal chicken souvlaki, halal yeero meat, falafel or halal roasted leg of lamb.

Greek fries & more

  • Greek Fries

    French fries topped with feta cheese and Greek seasoning.

  • Yeero Fries & Falafel Fries

    Fries loaded with yeero meat or falafel, feta, tzatziki and our Greek-style pico.

  • Greek Lamb Burger

    Our 50/50 lamb and beef burger on a toasted brioche bun with feta, greens, tomato, red onion and tzatziki.

  • Baklava

    Layers of fillo and walnuts soaked in a honey lemon syrup.
